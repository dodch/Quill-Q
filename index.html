    Apple requires web apps to be added to your Home Screen before allowing push notifications. Follow these 3 quick steps:

    1

    Tap the Share Button

    2

    Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

    3

    Open from Home Screen & Enable

    Open Quill-Q from your iPhone screen and tap "Enable Notifications".
